Pasta, one shape at a time

Four classic recipes, each built around the pasta that suits it best. Pick a shape and start cooking.

  • Spaghetti

Spaghetti carbonara

Silky egg and cheese sauce, crisp pork, and plenty of black pepper. There is no cream in a real carbonara.

Ingredients

  • 200 g spaghetti
  • 100 g guanciale or pancetta, diced
  • 2 whole eggs and 1 egg yolk
  • 50 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
  • Black pepper and salt

Method

  1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until just al dente. Save a cup of the cooking water.
  2. Fry the guanciale in a cold pan over medium heat until golden and crisp.
  3. Whisk the eggs, yolk, Pecorino and a generous amount of pepper in a bowl.
  4. Toss the drained pasta with the guanciale off the heat. Stir in the egg mixture with a splash of cooking water until glossy.

Penne all'arrabbiata

The name means angry, and the chilli is why. The ridges and hollow tubes of penne hold on to every drop of sauce.

Ingredients

  • 200 g penne
  • 400 g canned whole tomatoes, crushed by hand
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 to 2 dried chillies, crumbled
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh basil or parsley, and salt

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil with the garlic and chilli over low heat for 2 minutes. Do not let the garlic brown.
  2. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Simmer for 15 minutes until thick.
  3. Boil the penne until al dente, then drain.
  4. Toss the pasta in the sauce for a minute and finish with the fresh herbs.

Fettuccine Alfredo

Ribbons of egg pasta in a rich butter and Parmesan sauce. Good cheese matters more than anything else here.

Ingredients

  • 200 g fettuccine
  • 40 g butter
  • 60 g Parmesan, finely grated
  • 80 ml cream
  • Black pepper, a pinch of nutmeg, salt

Method

  1. Boil the fettuccine in salted water until al dente. Save a cup of the cooking water.
  2. Melt the butter with the cream in a wide pan over low heat.
  3. Add the drained pasta and toss. Sprinkle in the Parmesan a little at a time, loosening with cooking water.
  4. Finish with pepper and nutmeg, and serve straight away.

Fusilli al pesto

The spirals of fusilli trap fresh basil pesto in every twist. Make the pesto while the water comes to the boil.

Ingredients

  • 200 g fusilli
  • 50 g fresh basil leaves
  • 30 g pine nuts, lightly toasted
  • 40 g Parmesan, grated
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 80 ml olive oil and a pinch of salt

Method

  1. Blend the basil, pine nuts, garlic, Parmesan and salt, adding the olive oil slowly until it forms a rough paste.
  2. Boil the fusilli in salted water until al dente. Save a cup of the cooking water.
  3. Toss the drained pasta with the pesto and a splash of cooking water until every spiral is coated.
  4. Serve at once with extra Parmesan on top.
